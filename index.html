Common Questions

FAQ

How does mobile detailing work?

+

We bring everything — water, power, equipment, and premium products — right to your location. Just book a time and give us an address. You don't need to be home; just make sure we can access the vehicle.

How long does a detail take?

+

An exterior wash takes about 45 minutes. A full interior + exterior detail typically takes 2–3 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. We'll text you when we're done.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

+

Yes — just text us at least 24 hours before your appointment and we'll reschedule at no charge.

What if it rains?

+

If rain is in the forecast, we'll reach out to reschedule. We can still do interior-only details in covered areas like garages.
